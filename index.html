Lighthouse

Push your luck. Watch for the light.

How to Play

Goal: have the highest score once someone passes 100 points, without wrecking on the Lighthouses.

On your turn you roll 3 dice. Each die shows a number or a Lighthouse.

  • No Lighthouses: the numbers are added to your turn total. Then Bank it to your score and pass the device, or Roll Again for more.
  • 1 Lighthouse – Too close to the rocks! You lose this turn's points. Your score is safe. Turn over.
  • 2 Lighthouses – Risky water! You lose all your points, not just this turn's. Turn over.
  • 3 Lighthouses – Shipwrecked! Your score resets to 0 and you're out of the game.

Ending: when a player banks their way past 100, every other player gets one final turn to catch up. The highest score then wins. If everyone else is shipwrecked, the last ship afloat wins.

Playing solo? Set the player count to 1 when setting up to race Skipper, a bot that rolls and banks on its own.
